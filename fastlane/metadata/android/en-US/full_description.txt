Sleepy is a simple sleep diary that keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no cloud, no ads and no analytics, and the app does not even request the INTERNET permission.

<b>What you can do</b>
• Record a before-bed entry and a morning entry for each night
• Log night awakenings, sleep quality and notes
• Browse and edit your history
• See trends over time
• Type a quick note and let the built-in on-device parser fill in the form (this is a local parser, not cloud AI)
• Export and import JSON backups using Android's system file picker
• Optional daily bedtime and morning reminder notifications, delivered even when the app is closed (no internet or Google services needed)
• Home-screen shortcuts to log before bed or in the morning

<b>Privacy</b>
• All diary data is stored locally on the device
• No INTERNET permission, no trackers, no third-party services
• Only the sleep information you report is recorded; nothing is inferred
• Android auto-backup is disabled, so export a backup before changing phones

<b>Good to know</b>
Uninstalling the app deletes its data. Use Export backup regularly and keep the file somewhere safe.
